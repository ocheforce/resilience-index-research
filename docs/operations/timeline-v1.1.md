The Resilience Index

Pilot Project Timeline — Submission Copy

Submission Copy — October 2026

Duration: 6 months from funding release. Month 1 begins in the first month after funds are released.

Pre-funding

Submit the ethics application where permitted; time-stamp the prespecified protocol; confirm the clinical site; identify the research assistant and biostatistician; compile competition calendars.

Month 1 — Setup

Procure and calibrate analyzers and HRV devices; obtain testing materials; train the research assistant; configure KoboToolbox and the independent recruitment gatekeeper; complete a technical dry run.

Month 2 — Recruitment

Launch recruitment after ethics approval; screen and consent eligible participants; assign participant IDs; schedule baseline assessments.

Months 2–4 — Data Collection

Run four touchpoints per participant in overlapping waves; conduct safety monitoring and incidental-finding notifications; document withdrawals and reasons.

Month 5 — Analysis

Lock and quality-control the dataset; conduct the primary regression, Models A–F, sensitivity analyses and prespecified falsification assessment; obtain statistical methodological review.

Month 6 — Reporting

Prepare the results report, including null or negative findings; submit the preprint; deliver the funder report; prepare follow-on study planning.

Decision Gates

Before participant contact: ethics approval must be received.

End of Month 1: equipment calibrated, research assistant trained, technical dry run completed.

End of Month 2: target 120–150 participants consented; extend recruitment if substantially below target.

End of Month 4: target approximately 100 completers; close data collection.

End of Month 5: dataset locked and primary analyses completed.

End of Month 6: preprint submitted and funder report delivered.

Assumptions

Each participant requires approximately 15 days from baseline to recovery check. Data collection therefore runs in overlapping waves. Calendar dates will be fixed after funding release. Reporting refers to the funder report and preprint submission, not guaranteed journal publication.
