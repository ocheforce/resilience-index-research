The Resilience Index

Dissemination and Impact Plan — Submission Copy

Submission Copy — October 2026

Principles

Report the result regardless of direction. A null or negative result is scientifically useful.

Open by default where participant confidentiality permits: make the protocol, scoring approach and aggregate findings publicly checkable.

Local benefit first: participating athletes, teams and the host research/clinical setting receive an accessible summary of findings.

Audiences and Outputs

Planned Channels

Open preprint submission to an appropriate biomedical preprint repository, subject to platform and institutional requirements.

Public repository containing the protocol, scoring formulas, data dictionary and reproducible analysis pipeline.

Public release of aggregated results; row-level de-identified data only where consistent with consent, ethics approval and data-protection requirements.

Funder report and local presentation to the host institution and participating teams.

What Will Not Be Claimed

Dissemination will not describe the Resilience Index as a diagnostic tool, a validated predictor of longevity, or a clinically validated product. Public communication will distinguish pilot evidence from future hypotheses and applications.

Longer-Term Impact Pathway

Stage 1 is to establish whether recovery capacity can be measured and tracked longitudinally. If supported, later research will investigate factors associated with impaired recovery and whether targeted interventions can improve recovery trajectories. Broader populations and independent replication will be pursued only after the pilot evidence supports those next steps.
