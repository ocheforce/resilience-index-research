Submission Copy — October 2026

Funding Request

Recommended funding request: ₦10,148,320 (approximately US$6,765 at the planning exchange rate of ₦1,500/US$). Naira amounts are the operational basis; USD equivalents are illustrative.

Budget Summary

Recommended Realistic Tier

Contingency and Procurement

A 12% contingency is applied to the Realistic-tier subtotal. It is intended to absorb modest price movement, replacement needs and unavoidable implementation variance. Vendor prices should be re-quoted before purchase.

Budget Rationale

The Realistic tier provides the operational redundancy required for the four-touchpoint pilot without purchasing capacity beyond the current study. The Lean tier creates a single-analyzer dependency; the Well-resourced tier adds capacity more appropriate after pilot validation.

The budget distinguishes available vendor/institutional pricing from planning assumptions whose final cost depends on procurement or institutional access.

Ethics and Institutional Pathway

Formal ethics approval is required before participant contact or data collection. The current plan is to work through an appropriate hospital-based Health Research Ethics Committee in Taraba State, subject to formal submission and approval. NIMR or IHVN are potential backup institutional pathways if the primary route is unavailable.
